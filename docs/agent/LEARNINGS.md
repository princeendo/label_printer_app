This document is a review queue, not authoritative instruction. Agents may add reusable observations discovered during normal work, but must not treat a candidate as a standing preference until it is reviewed and promoted.

## Candidate Template

### Short candidate title

- **Proposed preference:** Actionable default behavior.
- **Scope:** `GLOBAL`, `REPOSITORY-SPECIFIC`, or `CONTEXTUAL`.
- **Confidence:** `HIGH`, `MEDIUM`, or `LOW`.
- **Source/context:** Concise provenance without copying a transcript or sensitive information.
- **Why it may matter again:** The recurring question or avoided rework.
- **Status:** `CANDIDATE`, `CONFIRMED`, `PROMOTED`, or `REJECTED`.
